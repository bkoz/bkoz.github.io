About Bob

Professional

I currently hold a position as a Principal Solutions Architect at Red Hat where I help organizations evaluate enterprise application platforms to host cloud native applications. The customers I work with are looking to migrate workloads from traditional application servers to container platforms such as Kubernetes and OpenShift for improved security, agility and efficiency.

My prior work experience included working with 3D graphics as a solutions architect. I specialized in real-time rendering, volumetric visualization and GPU programming. You can explore my technical projects on the Projects page.

Personal Interests

When I'm not working or taking classes, I like to study and play guitar with other members of Austin Classical Guitar. We rehearse weekly and perform recitals in Fall and Spring. These concerts are free to the public, so I hope you'll come out and see us sometime.

I've also attended the Big Jersey Guitar Camp and look forward to future guitar workshops and events.
